### Sequencing reads
I obtained sequencing reads from SRA: ERR13818447. This sequencing was conducted at the Monash Institute of Medical Research and used Illumina NovaSeq 6000 paired-end sequencing with a result of 81.9M bases, published on 2025-06-05. I pulled these directly into O2 using ncbi_datasets_cli for my analysis.
